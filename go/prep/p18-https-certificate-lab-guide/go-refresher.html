Go and subject refresher

Keep the certificate and private key outside the image and repository. A SAN for localhost proves the development hostname; clients must trust the development certificate explicitly.

tlsConfig := &tls.Config{MinVersion: tls.VersionTLS12}

Go implementation notes

  • Generate the development SAN certificate with OpenSSL and document trust separately from certificate creation.
  • Use http.Server with TLSConfig.MinVersion=tls.VersionTLS12 and current safe defaults; never use InsecureSkipVerify to make a test pass.
  • Mount private keys read-only, verify permissions for the non-root process, and test Secure cookies and shutdown over HTTPS.
