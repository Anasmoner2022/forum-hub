Overview

Containerize the working mini board and prove that its database survives container replacement and can be restored from a consistent backup.

Concrete deliverables

  • Dockerfile, .dockerignore and compose.yaml for one application instance
  • A build, replace, backup and restore demonstration on a clean machine

Why build this?

Make the “Clean build” case observable and demonstrate the failure or boundary case “Restore”. A peer must reproduce the artifact, not just recognize an API name.

Prerequisites

  • P11: Failure Handling, Tests and Team Integration

Reuse and what changes

  • P11: reuse its tested Go contracts; preserve their regression cases while adapting them to this project.

Time and team

Plan about 12 focused learner-hours including reading, building and peer review.
