Engineering questions & Socratic vision

  1. Why is a volume different from a container layer?
  2. Which files must travel together in a WAL database directory?
  3. Why can a CGO build succeed locally but its Linux container fail?
  4. What should happen when mounted storage is read-only?
  5. How would multiple application replicas change SQLite assumptions?
  6. What evidence proves a backup is useful rather than merely present?

Argue alternative designs and give a consequence that a reviewer could observe. These are discussion prompts without hidden model answers.
