Overview

Build a permission gateway for guest, member, moderator and administrator roles and prove that direct requests cannot bypass it.

Concrete deliverables

  • A route/action/resource permission matrix and enforcement module
  • A cross-role direct-request test suite

Why build this?

Make the “Guest write” case observable and demonstrate the failure or boundary case “Last admin”. A peer must reproduce the artifact, not just recognize an API name.

Prerequisites

  • P10: Session Cookies, Route Guards and CSRF
  • P11: Failure Handling, Tests and Team Integration

Reuse and what changes

  • P10: reuse its tested Go contracts; preserve their regression cases while adapting them to this project.
  • P11: reuse its tested Go contracts; preserve their regression cases while adapting them to this project.

Time and team

Plan about 10 focused learner-hours including reading, building and peer review.
