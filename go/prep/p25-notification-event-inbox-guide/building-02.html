Step 2: Persist and deliver idempotently

Write mutation+event and delivery+marker transactions with unique keys.

Questions before writing

  1. What enters this component of Transactional Events and Notification Inbox, and which fields are trusted?
  2. Which invariant survives a failure?
  3. Do not send a notification before the domain transaction commits.

Guided build

  1. Write an independent acceptance example for persist and deliver idempotently.
  2. Do not send a notification before the domain transaction commits.
  3. A dispatcher crash can happen after inserting the notification but before the marker unless both are atomic.
  4. Keep notification insertion and delivered-marker update in one sql.Tx with a unique event-recipient constraint and a batch limit of 100.
  5. Implement the smallest typed boundary and record the policy you chose.

Self-check

Retry a batch after an injected fault: every committed event has at most one recipient notification.

Evidence to keep

Keep exact input, result and state/diagram evidence. If the self-check fails, trace this component before moving on.
