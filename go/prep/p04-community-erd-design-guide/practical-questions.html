Practical questions

  1. Draw an example with two labels on one topic.
  2. List candidate keys for members.
  3. Add the no-label rejection to the scenario table.
  4. Compare your diagram to another team’s without sharing SQL.
  5. Trace deletion of a topic across every relation.

Use the finished artifact and record exact input, observed output and state. Keep these tasks small enough to demonstrate during peer review.
