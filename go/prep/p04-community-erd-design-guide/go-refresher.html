ER and relational modeling refresher

An ERD describes entities, participation and cardinality independently of the programming language. Derive keys and junction tables before choosing Go storage structs.

Go implementation notes

  • Keep the ERD independent of Go structs; a pointer field is not a database nullability policy.
  • Document the unique target/member reaction key and the one-active-session rule in the schema.
  • A database/sql adapter will implement these rules in P05–P07; distinguish a foreign key from a multi-row invariant.
