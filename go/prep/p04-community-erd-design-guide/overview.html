Overview

Design a community database from a requirements brief before writing SQL: an ERD, relational schema, data dictionary and constraint rationale.

Concrete deliverables

  • An ERD with keys, cardinalities and participation constraints
  • A relational schema, data dictionary and scenario-to-constraint table

Why build this?

Make the “ERD completeness” case observable and demonstrate the failure or boundary case “Minimum label count”. A peer must reproduce the artifact, not just recognize an API name.

Prerequisites

  • P03: HTML Forms and Safe Rendering Board

Reuse and what changes

  • P03: reuse its tested Go contracts; preserve their regression cases while adapting them to this project.

Time and team

Plan about 12 focused learner-hours including reading, building and peer review.
