Overview

Integrate guest/member/moderator/admin permissions, prepublication approval, role requests, reports, responses and category management into the forum.

Concrete deliverables

  • A moderation queue, member role-request form and admin desk
  • Permission matrix, workflow migration and direct-request audit suite

Why build this?

Make the “Guest/member separation” case observable and demonstrate the failure or boundary case “Conflict/leak audit”. A peer must reproduce the artifact, not just recognize an API name.

Prerequisites

  • F01: Forum: Full Stack Community
  • P22: Roles, Ownership and Permission Matrix
  • P23: Approval Queue, Role Requests, Reports and Categories

Reuse and what changes

  • F01: reuse its tested Go contracts; preserve their regression cases while adapting them to this project.
  • P22: reuse its tested Go contracts; preserve their regression cases while adapting them to this project.
  • P23: reuse its tested Go contracts; preserve their regression cases while adapting them to this project.

Time and team
