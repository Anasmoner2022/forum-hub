Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. How can a login limiter be abused to lock out a victim?
  2. Why do IP and identifier budgets need different purposes?
  3. What changes after a process restart?
  4. How should overload differ from incorrect credentials?
  5. Which limiter fields are safe to log?
  6. What fails if two replicas each keep their own counter?
