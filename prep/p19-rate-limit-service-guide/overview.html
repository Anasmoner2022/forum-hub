Overview

Build deterministic rate limiting for login and write routes, including memory cleanup and protection of expensive password verification.

Concrete deliverables

  • A limiter with injected clock and route policy table
  • Boundary and concurrent-request tests for 429 and Retry-After

Why build this?

The learner must make identifier limit observable and defend the failure case “Work bound”. Reading alone does not prove those behaviors; the artifact and review evidence do.

Prerequisites

  • P11: Failure Handling, Tests and Team Integration
  • P09: Registration and Password Hashing Vault

Reuse and what changes

  • P11: Failure Handling, Tests and Team Integration supplies tested contracts. Preserve those behaviors while building Rate Limiting and Bounded Work.
  • P09: Registration and Password Hashing Vault supplies tested contracts. Preserve those behaviors while building Rate Limiting and Bounded Work.

Time and team

Estimated 10 focused learner-hours, including reading, implementation and review. Each learner builds and explains the core; pairing and review are encouraged.
