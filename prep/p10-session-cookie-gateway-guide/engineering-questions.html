Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. Why should one active session be a database invariant?
  2. How is a pre-login CSRF token different from a logged-in session?
  3. What does HttpOnly protect and what does it leave exposed?
  4. Why should role information not live in a client cookie?
  5. How should logout behave if the browser already lacks a session?
  6. Which origin and redirect rules interact with future OAuth callbacks?
