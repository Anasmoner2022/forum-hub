Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. Why should filter combinations be specified before SQL?
  2. Can LEFT JOIN conditions accidentally remove empty topics?
  3. What changes if liked includes reply reactions?
  4. How does a deterministic tie-breaker affect page boundaries?
  5. Why should a filter not trust a supplied member ID?
  6. Which index would you retain if write cost mattered more than read speed?
