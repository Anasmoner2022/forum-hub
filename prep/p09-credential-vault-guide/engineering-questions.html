Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. Why is a random salt stored beside the hash?
  2. What tradeoff does a work factor create for legitimate users?
  3. Should failed verification use the same work as an unknown email?
  4. How might logs defeat otherwise correct password storage?
  5. Why should algorithm parameters be stored in the record?
  6. How would the service migrate old hashes after a successful login?
