Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. Why is OIDC identity different from an OAuth access token?
  2. Should provider email verification replace local identity policy?
  3. How can automatic email linking become account takeover?
  4. Which local fields remain editable after SSO onboarding?
  5. What happens if a provider changes its signing keys?
  6. What evidence should authorize attaching a provider to an existing account?
