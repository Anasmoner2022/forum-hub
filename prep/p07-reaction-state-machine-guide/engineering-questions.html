Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. Why is set-state safer than toggle for retries?
  2. Can a precheck replace a unique constraint?
  3. What is the unit of atomicity for a reaction?
  4. How should a UI behave after a retriable lock error?
  5. Would stored counters simplify or complicate integrity?
  6. How can a single event loop still encounter concurrent database writers?
