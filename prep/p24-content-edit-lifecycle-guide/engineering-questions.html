Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. What should the UI offer after a stale-write conflict?
  2. Why is an ownership precheck insufficient if the row changes before update?
  3. What does soft deletion cost in every read query?
  4. How should a notification link behave after deletion?
  5. Which audit facts survive content edits?
  6. When should deleting a post remove its files physically?
