Keep the boundary visible

  • editOwnedComment(db, actor, commentId, body, expectedVersion): Apply owner edit or forbidden/conflict.
  • deleteOwnedContent(db, actor, target, expectedVersion): Soft-delete and enforce child visibility consistently.
