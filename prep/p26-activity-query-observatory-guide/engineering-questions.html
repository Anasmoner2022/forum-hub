Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. Should activity display current state or a historical timeline?
  2. What does the viewer need to understand a comment entry?
  3. How can an activity page leak content hidden elsewhere?
  4. What is a useful index versus a redundant one?
  5. When does a single large UNION complicate pagination?
  6. How would deletion retention affect privacy and query cost?
