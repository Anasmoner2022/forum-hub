Overview

Build a permission gateway for guest, member, moderator and administrator roles and prove that direct requests cannot bypass it.

Concrete deliverables

  • A route/action/resource permission matrix and enforcement module
  • A cross-role direct-request test suite

Why build this?

The learner must make guest write observable and defend the failure case “Last admin”. Reading alone does not prove those behaviors; the artifact and review evidence do.

Prerequisites

  • P10: Session Cookies, Route Guards and CSRF
  • P11: Failure Handling, Tests and Team Integration

Reuse and what changes

  • P10: Session Cookies, Route Guards and CSRF supplies tested contracts. Preserve those behaviors while building Roles, Ownership and Permission Matrix.
  • P11: Failure Handling, Tests and Team Integration supplies tested contracts. Preserve those behaviors while building Roles, Ownership and Permission Matrix.

Time and team

Estimated 10 focused learner-hours, including reading, implementation and review. Each learner builds and explains the core; pairing and review are encouraged.
