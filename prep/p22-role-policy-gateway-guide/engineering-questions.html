Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. When does ownership matter in addition to role?
  2. Why should unknown actions default to deny?
  3. What is dangerous about caching a role in a long-lived session?
  4. How can an admin bootstrap become a backdoor?
  5. Should forbidden reads return 403 or 404?
  6. What does atomicity protect in the final-admin rule?
