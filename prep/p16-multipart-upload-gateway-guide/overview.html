Overview

Build an authenticated multipart intake service that limits bytes during receipt and cleans up every failed upload.

Concrete deliverables

  • A native form and Busboy-based streaming intake service
  • Boundary-byte fixtures and aborted-upload cleanup tests

Why build this?

The learner must make at limit observable and defend the failure case “Guest”. Reading alone does not prove those behaviors; the artifact and review evidence do.

Prerequisites

  • P10: Session Cookies, Route Guards and CSRF
  • P12: Docker Images, Volumes and Restore Drill

Reuse and what changes

  • P10: Session Cookies, Route Guards and CSRF supplies tested contracts. Preserve those behaviors while building Streaming Multipart Upload Gateway.
  • P12: Docker Images, Volumes and Restore Drill supplies tested contracts. Preserve those behaviors while building Streaming Multipart Upload Gateway.

Time and team

Estimated 10 focused learner-hours, including reading, implementation and review. Each learner builds and explains the core; pairing and review are encouraged.
