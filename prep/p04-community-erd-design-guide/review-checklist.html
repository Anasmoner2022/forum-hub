Exit gate

  • A peer can derive the same schema from the diagram without missing cardinalities.
  • Every rejection scenario identifies a real enforceable rule.
  • No supplied TODO remains in the final worksheet.

Do not start the next forum assignment merely because time elapsed. Resolve a failed readiness gate in preparation first.
