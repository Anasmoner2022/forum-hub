Overview

Design a community database from a requirements brief before writing SQL: an ERD, relational schema, data dictionary and constraint rationale.

Concrete deliverables

  • An ERD with keys, cardinalities and participation constraints
  • A relational schema, data dictionary and scenario-to-constraint table

Why build this?

The learner must make erd completeness observable and defend the failure case “Minimum label count”. Reading alone does not prove those behaviors; the artifact and review evidence do.

Prerequisites

  • P03: HTML Forms and Safe Rendering Board

Reuse and what changes

  • P03: HTML Forms and Safe Rendering Board supplies tested contracts. Preserve those behaviors while building Community ERD and Relational Schema Design.

Time and team

Estimated 12 focused learner-hours, including reading, implementation and review. Each learner builds and explains the core; pairing and review are encouraged.
