Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. Which forum values are derived rather than stored facts?
  2. What does an optional foreign key mean in your ERD?
  3. Would separate reaction tables simplify the target constraint?
  4. What happens to reply history when a member is deleted?
  5. Why is at least one label harder than at most one active session?
  6. Which future extension would most stress this schema?
