Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. What is the difference between a useful failure test and a mirror of the code?
  2. Which failures may a client safely retry?
  3. Should logs contain user-submitted content?
  4. How can temporary database tests still share state accidentally?
  5. What evidence distinguishes a handled exception from a swallowed one?
  6. Which work should a team share rather than divide by layer?
