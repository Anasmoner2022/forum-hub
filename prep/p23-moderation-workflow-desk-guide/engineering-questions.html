Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. Should rejected posts be editable or require a new submission?
  2. Who should see a moderation reason?
  3. How can a deleted target remain meaningful in a report?
  4. Why prohibit self-approval?
  5. Would reassigning categories be safer than refusing deletion?
  6. What must be atomic when a role request is approved?
