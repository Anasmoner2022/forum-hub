Scope boundary

The no-new-concept policy applies to the required Node.js learning route. It means learners practise a skill and pass a readiness check before its capstone. Integration still involves new combinations and decisions. Any newly required library, infrastructure, transport or bonus needs preparation first. Go teams pass equivalent behavioral gates with their selected implementation stack.

Learn it. Build it. Explain it.
