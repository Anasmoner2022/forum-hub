Overview

Integrate durable notifications, private personal activity and owner editing/deletion without duplicating events, leaking content or losing concurrent edits.

Concrete deliverables

  • A notification inbox, personal activity sections and edit/delete pages
  • Event, lifecycle and query-plan evidence plus regression tests

Why build this?

The learner must make comment/like/dislike observable and defend the failure case “Rollback/index regression”. Reading alone does not prove those behaviors; the artifact and review evidence do.

Prerequisites

  • F01: Forum: Full Stack Community
  • P24: Owned Editing, Deletion and Stale Update Conflicts
  • P25: Transactional Events and Notification Inbox
  • P26: Personal Activity Queries and Index Observatory

Reuse and what changes

  • P24: Owned Editing, Deletion and Stale Update Conflicts supplies tested contracts. Preserve those behaviors while building Forum Advanced Features: Notifications, Activity and Editing.

Time and team

Optional assignment, 1 week, team of 4–5. This time begins after readiness review; preparation is additional.

A team of 4–5 students chooses one backend for the shared forum repository: JavaScript on Node.js or Go. The choice applies consistently to the base and its extensions. The behavioral contract and assessment are the same. These guided chapters teach the Node.js route. Go teams must satisfy the equivalent preparation checks before starting and already know Go syntax, modules, errors and its standard library.

Node route: core node:http/node:https, node:sqlite, node:crypto, node:test and semantic HTML. No frontend frameworks or ORM; Busboy, Sharp and jose are allowed for prepared specialist tasks. Go route: net/http, html/template, database/sql with an approved SQLite driver, crypto/rand, a reviewed password-hash package and testing. See the language policy for exact package and readiness rules.
