Engineering questions & Socratic vision

Discuss these with your team after building. There is no hidden answer or single correct architecture; use your artifact and evidence to defend your choice.

  1. Which threats remain after HTTPS is correctly configured?
  2. How should the team tune expensive password work without weakening records?
  3. What limits can themselves harm legitimate users?
  4. How do session rotation and role changes interact?
  5. Which proxy assumptions are currently safe to make?
  6. What distinguishes attack evidence from a list of installed defenses?
